      A PRACTICAL WAY TO WORK

      Good work starts
      with understanding.

      A clear scope. Something you can see early. A result you know how to use.

      1. 01 / LISTEN

        Understand the real task.

        What happens today, what gets in the way, and what should be different?

      2. 02 / MAKE IT VISIBLE

        Start with a small example.

        A workflow map, a design direction, or a focused prototype to respond to.

      3. 03 / BUILD & REFINE

        Keep you in the loop.

        Review the work against the agreed scope and test the important details.

      4. 04 / HAND OVER

        Make the next step yours.

        Receive the agreed files, instructions, and clarity about any running costs.

      A few good questions.

      Can we start with something small?+

      Yes. A single process, one landing page, or a set of visuals is a useful starting point. We’ll agree on what’s included before work begins.

      Will an AI workflow need paid tools?+

      It depends on the job and your existing tools. Any hosting, model, or other service costs should be identified in the project scope before you commit.

      Are these projects client work?+

      The AI projects link to my public professional background and open-source work. The landing page and graphics are original design concepts, and the enquiry tool is a guided sample. Each project is labeled so you can see its context.

      What should I bring to a first conversation?+

      Your goal, an example of the current process or design, the tools you use, and a rough timeline. You don’t need a perfect brief to get started.
